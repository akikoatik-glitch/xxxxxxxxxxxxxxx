About XWhiz

By XWhiz Data Team · Updated 2026-09-04 · Model Dixon-Coles v3

XWhiz is an independent football predictions site. Every day our model analyses real fixtures and publishes 1X2, Over/Under 2.5, BTTS and most-likely-score probabilities — free, with no account and no invented matches.

Who runs XWhiz

XWhiz is maintained by the XWhiz Data Team. The team builds and operates the full prediction pipeline: data ingestion from public sports-data feeds, the Dixon-Coles + Elo statistical model, and this website. Editorial responsibility for every published prediction and page sits with the team as a whole. We do not publish anonymous tips or use ghost authors.

How predictions are generated

Every prediction comes from a quantitative model. Team strength is a blend of Elo ratings and current-season attack/defence form. Expected goals feed a Dixon-Coles adjusted Poisson process and produce a full 0–6 score matrix. All market probabilities — 1X2, Over/Under 2.5, BTTS, correct score — are derived from that single matrix. Full details are on the methodology page.

Data sources

Fixtures and historical results are sourced from public sports-data feeds including football-data.org and openfootball/football.json. We only ever publish predictions for matches with a real external fixture identifier — never invented or placeholder fixtures.

How we are funded

XWhiz is free to use and affiliate-funded. Pages may contain Melbet affiliate links. These are always labelled with rel="sponsored", opened in a new tab, and disclosed in this footer and on each match page. If you register via those links we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Affiliate revenue does not and cannot influence model output. The model computes probabilities first; the editorial team decides what to publish. Affiliate selection happens after that.

Corrections and updates

If a published pick is found to be based on incorrect data we will update it as soon as possible and note the correction. The model regenerates daily at 06:00 UTC so historical picks do not silently change.
