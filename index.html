About today's football predictions

XWhiz produces football predictions every day from a Dixon-Coles adjusted Poisson model combined with Elo ratings, using real fixtures from public sports data. Every pick includes a 1X2 recommendation, expected goals, Over/Under 2.5 and BTTS probabilities, plus the most likely correct score.

Why the model varies scores naturally

We never fabricate a scoreline. The most likely score is the one with the highest probability inside the model's score matrix, so you see natural variety — 2-0, 1-2, 3-1, 2-2 — instead of forced or repeated 1-1s. Predictions refresh daily at 06:00 UTC.
