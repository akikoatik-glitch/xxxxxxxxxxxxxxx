Zirconia · CAD/CAM Texas

Zirconia restorations with controlled sintering

Multilayer blanks for gradient shade + strength. Nesting, milling, sintering and finishing are tracked per case — the buyer adds puck brands, furnace protocols and warranty terms.

From puck to polish

1. STL intake & margin marking → 2. CAD anatomy + cutback → 3. Nesting in multilayer puck → 4. 5-axis mill → 5. Sinter (e.g. ~1530°C cycle placeholder) → 6. Stain/glaze → 7. QC on model. No material superiority claims — just a transparent pipeline.
