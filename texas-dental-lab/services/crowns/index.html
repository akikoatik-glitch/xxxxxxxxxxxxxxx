Crowns · Texas dental lab

Dental crowns, designed for fit and light

Monolithic zirconia for posterior strength, layered ceramic for anterior lifelikeness. Every crown starts from your STL — margins marked digitally, contacts and occlusion designed before milling.

Options the buyer can publish

  • ✓
    Layered / glass-ceramic

    Cutback + porcelain for demanding anterior cases. Placeholder for e.max-style lineup.

  • ✓
    What to send

    STL/PLY, shade photos (with tab), bite, prep photos, desired cement gap note.

Why dentists reorder crowns

Seating confidence comes from margins you can see on the model and contacts that need minimal adjustment. This concept promises process — not outcomes.
