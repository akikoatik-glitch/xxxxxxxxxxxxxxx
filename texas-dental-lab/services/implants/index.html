Implants · CAD/CAM Texas

Implant restorations planned from the scan

Scan bodies, Ti-bases, custom abutments and screw-retained crowns. Emergence, angulation and occlusion are designed virtually — the buyer adds supported implant systems and torque protocols.

What to send

STL with scan bodies, opposing + bite, implant system + platform size, shade photos, screw-channel preference, torque value note. Full-arch concepts are quoted per case — no outcome promises, only a verifiable design file trail.

Implant checklist (concept)

System · platform · Ti-base · abutment type · retention · shade · occlusion scheme.
